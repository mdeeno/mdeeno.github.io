# High Traffic Channel Activation Meeting

Date: 2026-06-19
Channel: GitHub Pages blog `tech.mdeeno.com`
Goal: Grow qualified organic traffic and convert it into M-DEENO owned-channel leads.

## Meeting Decision

The blog will operate as a high-intent acquisition channel, not a broad real-estate news archive.

Traffic growth will focus on pages where the reader has an immediate reason to use M-DEENO:

- Reconstruction contribution risk
- Construction cost increase
- Ratio/profitability scenarios
- Interest rate, LTV, DSR, jeonse pressure
- Specific complex or district risk checks
- Calculator-assisted decision points

## Source Ground Rules

Google's official guidance confirms that generative AI visibility is still grounded in search quality and foundational SEO:

- SEO must help search engines crawl, index, understand, and present useful content.
- Generative AI search uses content from the Search index, so AI/GEO optimization does not replace SEO.
- Structured data should be implemented only where it accurately represents visible page content.

References:

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data

## Activation Model

Use a four-layer traffic model.

Layer 1: Indexable Pillar Pages

- `재건축 분담금 계산`
- `비례율이 낮으면 분담금이 얼마나 늘어나는가`
- `공사비 900만 원 시대 재건축 생존 전략`
- `이주비 대출 DSR/LTV 한도`
- `1기 신도시 단지별 분담금 리스크`

Layer 2: Weekly Issue Pages

- News-triggered posts remain useful only if they explain the risk mechanism.
- Every issue post must link back to one pillar page, one calculator, and one free diagnosis CTA.

Layer 3: Complex Pages

- Use complex pages as bottom-of-funnel SEO pages.
- Link from market posts to relevant complex pages whenever a region or complex is mentioned.

Layer 4: Conversion Paths

- Top CTA: free diagnosis
- Mid CTA: calculator or related complex page
- Bottom CTA: free diagnosis with UTM
- Newsletter/form capture where the reader is not ready for diagnosis

## Weekly Publishing Cadence

Minimum:

- 2 market/risk posts per week
- 1 evergreen pillar/support post per week
- 5 internal link updates per week

Do not publish generic market commentary unless it ties to:

- A measurable risk
- A calculator
- A specific complex or district
- A direct diagnosis CTA
